SOP Manual AI prompt

Let's write a manual someone else can follow step by step.

Copy this prompt and paste it as is into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI chat. Nothing to fill in first: the AI asks the questions below, and once you answer, it writes the result.

What the AI will ask you

  1. A manual for what task? Tell me how it is done, as far as you know.
  2. Who will use it?
  3. Format?

What you get

Sections: Overview / What you need (tools, access) / Step-by-step instructions (with screens or locations) / Common mistakes and tips / Who to contact when stuck. Mark steps I did not describe [TO CONFIRM].

Full prompt

Let's write a manual someone else can follow step by step.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) A manual for what task? Tell me how it is done, as far as you know.
2) Who will use it? (options: new hire / successor / another team; default: successor)
3) Format? (options: step-by-step / with checklist / table; default: step-by-step)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Sections: Overview / What you need (tools, access) / Step-by-step instructions (with screens or locations) / Common mistakes and tips / Who to contact when stuck. Mark steps I did not describe [TO CONFIRM].
