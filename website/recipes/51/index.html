RECIPE 51 · Marketing / Brand

Brand Naming & Slogan Candidates + Consumer A/B Survey

Copy 3 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Product Concept

Steps

  1. Button 17 · Naming Ideas

    Let's come up with names and slogans.

    See the prompt
    Let's come up with names and slogans.

Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What needs a name? Tell me what it does and the feeling you want.
2) Language? (options: Korean / English / both; default: both)
3) Tone? (options: trustworthy / friendly / sleek / playful; default: friendly)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Table of 8 to 10 candidates with columns: Name / Meaning / Matching slogan / Watch-outs (pronunciation, possible similar trademarks). Remind me to check trademarks and domains myself.
