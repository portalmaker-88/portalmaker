RECIPE 54 · Marketing / Brand

Global Target Audience Localization & Overseas Landing Page

Copy 3 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Domestic Campaign Plan

Steps

  1. Button 26 · Campaign Idea

    Let's plan marketing campaign ideas.

    See the prompt
    Let's plan marketing campaign ideas.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What are you promoting, and to whom?
2) Budget? (options: almost none / small / generous; default: small)
3) Main channels? (options: social media / offline / search and ads / your call; default: your call)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Write 2 or 3 campaign options. For each: Concept / Key message / Channels and execution / What makes customers take part / Success metrics. Recommend one that fits the budget.

  2. Button 50 · Global Localize

    Let's adapt this for an overseas market or another culture.

    See the prompt
    Let's adapt this for an overseas market or another culture.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What are we localizing? Paste the text or plan.
2) Target country or region? (options: US / Japan / Southeast Asia / Europe / other; default: US)
3) Output? (options: translated and adapted in the local language / adaptation notes in my language; default: translated and adapted)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Point out the cultural, custom, and regulatory differences that matter most, then show the adapted version. Go beyond translation: adjust expressions, examples, units, and date formats.

  3. Button 78 · Landing Page

    Let's write the structure and copy for a landing page.

    See the prompt
    Let's write the structure and copy for a landing page.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What do you sell, and to whom? Share features and price as far as you know.
2) Desired action? (options: buy / sign up for a free trial / request a consultation; default: request a consultation)
3) Any testimonials or results? (options: yes (paste them) / no; default: no)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Write it section by section: Hero (headline and button) / Customer problem / Solution / 3 key benefits / Proof (reviews, results) / FAQ / Final button. Do not invent reviews or results; leave [TESTIMONIAL PLACEHOLDER] if none.
