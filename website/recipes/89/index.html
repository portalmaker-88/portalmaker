RECIPE 89 · Office / Reporting

Long Document / Article 3-Line Executive Summary & Share Email

Copy 3 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Long Text Material

Steps

  1. Button 1 · Summarize

    Let me read your material and pull out what matters.

    See the prompt
    Let me read your material and pull out what matters.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Paste the material to summarize. (An email, document, article, meeting notes, anything.)
2) What is the summary for? (options: a quick read for me / reporting to my manager / sharing with my team; default: a quick read for me)
3) Length? (options: 3 lines / half a page / detailed by section; default: 3 lines)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Order: Key summary / Important numbers and dates / What I need to do or reply to / Anything unclear in the material.

  2. Button 51 · 3-Line Summary

    Let's cut it down to three lines.

    See the prompt
    Let's cut it down to three lines.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Paste the content to shorten. (If there is an earlier answer in this chat, I'll use that.)
2) Who will read it? (options: me / my manager / the whole team; default: my manager)
3) Emphasize? (options: conclusion / action items / numbers; default: conclusion)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Three lines, one sentence each. Line 1 is the conclusion; the others are reasons or actions. Add nothing that is not in the original.

  3. Button 68 · Biz Email

    Let's write a work email together.

    See the prompt
    Let's write a work email together.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Who is it to, and what do you need to tell or ask them? Include any deadline.
2) Your relationship to the recipient? (options: manager / another team / client; default: another team)
3) Tone? (options: polite / friendly / firm; default: polite)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Put the purpose and the request in the first two lines, and give the request a deadline. Give 2 subject lines and 1 body of 10 lines or fewer.
