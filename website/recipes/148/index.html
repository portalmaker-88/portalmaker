RECIPE 148 · Data / Automation

System Architecture & Logic Structure Mermaid Diagram Code

Copy 3 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Plan or Architecture

Steps

  1. Button 12 · Outline Setup

    Let's design the outline of your document.

    See the prompt
    Let's design the outline of your document.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What document is it? Tell me the topic and purpose.
2) Who will read it? (options: my manager / executives / clients / my team; default: my manager)
3) Length? (options: 1 to 2 pages / about 5 pages / 10+ pages; default: about 5 pages)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Split the outline into main, sub, and detailed headings, with one line on what goes in each. Put the conclusion or request near the front.

  2. Button 35 · MECE Struct

    Let's regroup the content with no overlaps or gaps (MECE).

    See the prompt
    Let's regroup the content with no overlaps or gaps (MECE).

1) Paste the content to organize. (If there is an earlier answer in this chat, I'll use that.)
2) Preferred grouping? (options: by topic / by time / by owner / your call; default: your call)
3) Output form? (options: numbered list / table / tree; default: numbered list)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Merge overlapping items and mark missing ones [SUGGESTED ADDITION]. Number the hierarchy so it is clear, and state the grouping rule in one line. Do not change the content itself.
