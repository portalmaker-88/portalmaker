RECIPE 62 · Marketing / Brand

Full-Funnel Customer Journey CRM Email Sequence

Copy 4 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Service/Product

Steps

  1. Button 15 · User Journey

    Let's map how customers find and use you (customer journey).

    See the prompt
    Let's map how customers find and use you (customer journey).

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Which product or service? Who are the main customers?
2) Scope? (options: first awareness to purchase / purchase to repeat use / the whole journey; default: the whole journey)
3) Any known pain points? (options: yes (tell me) / no; default: no)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Table by stage with columns: Stage / Customer action / Thoughts and feelings / Touchpoint (app, store, support, etc.) / Pain point / Improvement idea. Mark the 1 or 2 points to fix first.

  2. Button 18 · Copy Draft

    Let's write headlines and copy that grab attention.

    See the prompt
    Let's write headlines and copy that grab attention.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What are you promoting, and to whom?
2) Where will it run? (options: ads / product page / social media / email subject; default: social media)
3) Tone? (options: serious / friendly / playful; default: friendly)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Write 6 to 10 headline and sub-copy pairs. Mix angles such as curiosity, empathy, and benefit, and label the angle of each. Avoid unsupported claims like 'best' or 'No. 1'.

  3. Button 54 · Persuasion Up

    Let's make it more persuasive so the reader agrees and acts.

    See the prompt
    Let's make it more persuasive so the reader agrees and acts.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Paste the text. (If there is an earlier answer in this chat, I'll use that.)
2) Who must be persuaded? (options: my manager / customers / another team / investors; default: my manager)
3) Desired action? (options: approve / buy / cooperate / reply; default: approve)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Answer the reader's likely concerns first and bring evidence (cases, numbers, others' reactions) forward. End with a clear ask. Do not invent evidence; mark [ADD EVIDENCE]. Avoid pressure tactics or exaggeration.

  4. Button 68 · Biz Email

    Let's write a work email together.

    See the prompt
    Let's write a work email together.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. 1) Who is it to, and what do you need to tell or ask them? Include any deadline.
2) Your relationship to the recipient? (options: manager / another team / client; default: another team)
3) Tone? (options: polite / friendly / firm; default: polite)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Put the purpose and the request in the first two lines, and give the request a deadline. Give 2 subject lines and 1 body of 10 lines or fewer.
