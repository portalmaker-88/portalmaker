RECIPE 70 · Sales / CS

Multi-Stakeholder (C-Level vs. Working-Level) B2B Pitch Proposal

Copy 4 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Client Org Info

Steps

  1. Button 46 · Stakeholder

    Let's analyze the people involved (stakeholders) and how to win them over.

    See the prompt
    Let's analyze the people involved (stakeholders) and how to win them over.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What are you pushing forward? List the people or teams involved, as far as you know.
2) Who worries you most? (options: executives / other teams / frontline staff / outside parties; default: other teams)
3) Goal? (options: get approval / get cooperation / reduce opposition; default: get cooperation)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Stakeholder table: Who / What they want / Concerns or reasons to resist / Influence (H/M/L) / How to persuade / Who contacts them and when.

  2. Button 7 · Value Prop

    Let's pin down the core value you offer customers.

    See the prompt
    Let's pin down the core value you offer customers.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What do you offer, and to whom? One or two lines.
2) Main alternatives or competitors? (options: I'll tell you / not sure; default: not sure)
3) Where will this be used? (options: a plan / marketing copy / internal notes; default: a plan)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Match Customer problem / Our solution / Why it beats other options in a table, then write the value proposition in one sentence.

  3. Button 57 · Matrix Table

    Let's turn the content into an at-a-glance comparison table.

    See the prompt
    Let's turn the content into an at-a-glance comparison table.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Paste the content to convert. (If there is an earlier answer in this chat, I'll use that.)
2) Preferred comparison criteria (columns)? (options: I'll tell you / your call; default: your call)
3) Where will it be used? (options: report / paste into a spreadsheet / slides; default: report)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Rows are the items compared, columns the criteria. Mark empty cells '-' or [TO CONFIRM], and add a 1 to 2 line takeaway below the table.

  4. Button 64 · Biz Proposal

    Let's draft a proposal for a client or partner.

    See the prompt
    Let's draft a proposal for a client or partner.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. 1) Who are you proposing to, and what? Tell me what you know about their situation.
2) What do they care about most? (options: cost / results / reliability / speed; default: results)
3) Length? (options: 1-page summary / about 5 pages / detailed; default: about 5 pages)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Sections: Their problem / Our solution / Why us / Plan and timeline / Expected results / Cost (if I gave it) / Next steps. Use result figures only when I gave a basis; otherwise mark [TO CONFIRM].
