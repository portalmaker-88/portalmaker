RECIPE 25 · Product / PM

User Research Survey & In-Depth Interview (IDI) Guide

Copy 3 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Customer Need to Validate

Steps

  1. Button 5 · Target Persona

    Let's describe your core customers in concrete terms.

    See the prompt
    Let's describe your core customers in concrete terms.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Customers of which product or service? One or two lines.
2) Customer type? (options: consumers / business clients / internal staff; default: consumers)
3) Do you already know anything about them? (options: yes (tell me) / no; default: no)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Put 1 to 3 customer types in a table with columns: Who they are / Main frustrations / Why they buy or use it / What they look at when deciding / Where we reach them. Label anything not from my input as an assumption and add how to check it (interviews, survey, data).

  2. Button 24 · Survey Design

    Let's design your survey.

    See the prompt
    Let's design your survey.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What is the survey about, and who answers it?
2) How many questions? (options: about 5 / about 10 / about 20; default: about 10)
3) Format? (options: online link / in person / phone; default: online link)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Order: Screening / Core questions (attitudes, behavior) / Open feedback. Give each question its options and answer type (multiple choice, scale, open), and avoid leading questions. Add 2 to 3 lines on how to analyze the results.

  3. Button 87 · Interview Guide

    Let's build a structured interview guide.

    See the prompt
    Let's build a structured interview guide.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) Who are you interviewing, and what do you want to learn? Tell me the role or topic.
2) Type? (options: job interview / expert interview / customer interview; default: job interview)
3) Length? (options: 30 min / 1 hour; default: 1 hour)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Group into Competency / Situational / Behavioral questions, each with a follow-up. For job interviews add criteria for strong and weak answers, and avoid questions that are legally off-limits (age, marital status, etc.).
