RECIPE 50 · Marketing / Brand

Competitor USP Comparison Matrix & Marketing Proposal

Copy 4 buttons in order and paste each into the same AI chat. When the AI asks questions, answer them, then move on to the next button.

Have this answer ready

After you paste the first button, the AI asks for this: Our vs. Competitor Features

Steps

  1. Button 22 · Competitor Scan

    Let's map the competition.

    See the prompt
    Let's map the competition.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What do you do, and who do you think the competitors are? (If unsure, just name the field.)
2) Comparison criteria? (options: price / quality / convenience / brand / your call; default: your call)
3) Purpose? (options: find our edge / a report / set pricing; default: find our edge)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Compare direct competitors and substitutes in a table: Competitor / Strengths / Weaknesses / Why customers choose them. End with 1 or 2 gaps we can target. Mark unverified competitor facts [TO CONFIRM].

  2. Button 44 · USP Sharpening

    Let's sharpen what makes you clearly different.

    See the prompt
    Let's sharpen what makes you clearly different.

[Ask first] If this chat has no relevant content yet, do not start writing. Ask the questions below in one message, leaving out any already answered in this chat. Question 1 is open for me to answer; give questions 2 and 3 options and a suggested default. End with "You only need to answer question 1. Unless you say otherwise, I'll use the defaults for 2 and 3."
1) What are you presenting or proposing? Paste or summarize it.
2) Compared with what? (options: I'll tell you / typical alternatives; default: typical alternatives)
3) Who is it for? (options: customers / investors / internal; default: customers)

[Write] Once I answer, write it as follows. Where I say I don't know, state your assumption and continue. Do not invent numbers or facts I did not give; mark them [TO CONFIRM].
Pick 2 or 3 differentiators. For each: one-sentence message / supporting evidence / evidence still needed if weak. Avoid unsupported words like 'only' or 'best'.

    Let's turn the content into an at-a-glance comparison table.

3) Where will it be used? (options: report / paste into a spreadsheet / slides; default: report)

Rows are the items compared, columns the criteria. Mark empty cells '-' or [TO CONFIRM], and add a 1 to 2 line takeaway below the table.

    Let's draft a proposal for a client or partner.

    Let's draft a proposal for a client or partner.
